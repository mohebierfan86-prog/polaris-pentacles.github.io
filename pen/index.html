IV · The Work

One product, shipped end to end

PipelineHQ

A full-stack B2B sales CRM — Salesforce-lite for SaaS teams: capture leads, run deals through a gated pipeline, and hit forecast with real process enforcement.

Three roles share one workspace. Sales Development owns and converts leads. Account Executives move deals, complete MEDDIC, and close with win/loss reasons. Sales Managers run the admin hub, forecast, and report exports.

The product is not just CRUD screens: API-enforced stage gates, Celery background jobs, WebSocket alerts, lead routing, email sequences, global search, and analytics with CSV export — backed by Django REST, PostgreSQL, Redis, and a Next.js client.
